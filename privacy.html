गोपनीयता नीति · Privacy Policy

Om Japamala (np.japamala.app) · अन्तिम अद्यावधिक: असोज २९, २०८३ · Last updated: 15 October 2026

ॐ जपमाला मन्त्र जपको लागि बनाइएको एप हो। यसमा खाता, विज्ञापन वा ट्र्याकिङ छैन।

Om Japamala is an app for chanting mantras. It has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking.

१. फोनमै रहने जानकारी · Data kept on your phone

तपाईंको जप गन्ती, समय, सङ्कल्प, रोजेको ठाउँ र सेटिङ तपाईंकै फोनमा मात्र राखिन्छ। हामी यो कहीं पठाउँदैनौं। एप हटाउँदा यी सबै मेटिन्छन्।

Your japa counts, time spent, vows (sankalpa), chosen place and settings are stored only on your phone. We do not send them anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

२. माइक र रेकर्ड गरिएको आवाज · Microphone and recorded voices

“आवाज रेकर्ड गर्नुहोस्” प्रयोग गर्दा आवाज तपाईंको फोनमै एपभित्र राखिन्छ। यो कहीं अपलोड हुँदैन। तपाईं जुनसुकै बेला हटाउन सक्नुहुन्छ।

When you use “Record voice”, the recording is saved privately inside the app on your phone. It is never uploaded. You can remove it at any time.

३. बोलेर जाँच · Speak & check

“बोलेर जाँच” थिच्दा मात्र माइक खुल्छ। तपाईंको बोली फोनको बोली पहिचान सेवाले (Android मा प्रायः Google) शब्दमा बदल्छ। फोनमै गर्न नसकिँदा त्यो सेवाले आवाज आफ्नो सर्भरमा पठाउन सक्छ; त्यसमा Google को गोपनीयता नीति लागू हुन्छ। एपले केवल शब्द पाउँछ, मन्त्रसँग मिलाउँछ र राख्दैन।

The microphone is used only while “Speak & check” is on. Your phone’s speech recognition service (usually Google on Android) turns speech into words. When it cannot do this on the phone, that service may send the audio to its servers, under Google’s privacy policy. The app receives only the recognised words, compares them with the mantra, and does not store them.

४. फोनको आवाज · Phone voice

“Chant along” uses your phone’s built-in text-to-speech to read the mantra text aloud. Only the mantra text is given to it.

५. स्थान · Location

The app does not use GPS or location services. Sunrise and planetary-hour times are calculated on the phone for the place you pick from a list.

६. बालबालिका · Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
